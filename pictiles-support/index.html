PicTiles Support & Help

Welcome to the official support page for PicTiles: Photo Puzzle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I play the game?

A: Drag and drop tiles to restore the image. There's no timer — play at your own pace.

Q: How do I remove ads?

A: You can make a one-time purchase from the in-game settings to remove all ads permanently.

Q: The game is not loading – what can I do?

A: Make sure you have a stable internet connection. Restart the app and try again. If the issue persists, contact us below.
